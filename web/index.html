Put someone you love in the spotlight

Celebrations is a little shared stage for birthdays, farewells, thank-yous and wins. One person takes the stage; everyone else fills the audience and lines up to tell them something nice.

  1. Create a stage. Pick a scene (coder, biker, chef, DJ…) and design their character.
  2. Share the link. Everyone who opens it takes a seat with their own avatar.
  3. Line up with messages. Each one is delivered on stage, with a high five, a bow or a confetti blast, while the whole audience watches.
